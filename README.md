# Zenvy Daily Check-In | Pre-launch metrics framework

A NextLeap Business Analyst Fellowship case study on how to measure a proposed two-minute mental wellness feature before and after launch. The work is a **measurement plan**, not a report of observed product results.

## The question

How can Zenvy tell whether Daily Check-In helps people form a useful reflection habit, supports wellbeing, and contributes to retention and upgrades without overlooking signs of harm?

## Framework

- **North Star:** Weekly Habit Rate - the share of eligible feature-group users completing at least three check-ins in a week.
- **Drivers:** Starting, completing, and returning to Check-In. Screen drop-off, timing, and device behavior help explain changes.
- **Business outcomes:** Day-30 retention and free-to-premium upgrades, compared across randomly assigned feature and holdout groups.
- **Customer outcomes and guardrails:** Comparable wellbeing surveys in both groups, reminder opt-outs, uninstalls, and possible displacement of deeper journaling.
- **Launch measurement:** Event instrumentation, segment views, a shared dashboard, and explicit checks for the differences between the written flow and wireframes.

The proposal uses an eligible-user holdout rather than comparing only frequent Check-In users with everyone else. It separates a habit signal from evidence of wellbeing or business impact.
